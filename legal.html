4. Prices and payment

  • Booking prices are in euro (EUR) and payment is taken in euro.
  • Prices shown in other currencies, such as CHF, USD or GBP, are approximate and for reference only. Your bank may apply its own exchange rate.
  • The total price is confirmed to you before you pay. Fares and availability can change until the ticket is issued.
  • Payment methods: Visa and Mastercard cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay, bank transfer in euro, or payment at our office in Prishtina. BUNAVIA does not charge a service fee. The total shown before you pay is the price you pay. to confirm: payment provider

6. Changes and cancellations

Whether a ticket can be changed or cancelled, and any cost, depends on the airline’s fare rules for your ticket. Contact us as soon as possible and we will check the options and handle the request. 8. Changes made by the airline

Privacy policy

1. Who is responsible for your data

BUNAVIA L.L.C. (details in section 1 of the terms above) is the controller of the personal data described here. Contact for privacy questions: info@bunavia.com.

2. Whose data this covers

  • People who visit this website.
  • People who contact us or ask for fares, by form, email, phone or chat.
  • People who create a customer account on our website.
  • Customers and the passengers on a booking, once online booking is available.
  • Representatives of travel agencies and other businesses who ask to work with us.

3. What data we use

  • Contact details: name, company, email address, phone number and your message.
  • Account details: if you register, your name, email address, phone number, preferred language and password. Your password is stored only in encrypted (hashed) form and we cannot read it.
  • Booking details: passenger names, gender and dates of birth, contact email and phone, trip details, whether your dates are flexible, your baggage and seat choices, the payment method you choose, and, once tickets are issued, travel document details and ticket information.
  • Saved travellers: if you choose to save travellers in your account (for example family members), their names, gender and dates of birth, so that you can select them in later bookings. You can remove them at any time in My account; they are deleted with your account.
  • Travel insurance offer: if you tick the box during booking, we note that you want an insurance offer and contact you about it.
  • Special requests: anything you write in the requests field, for example a need for wheelchair assistance. This can reveal health information, so we use it only to arrange the service with the airline, and only with your consent given by entering it.
  • Invoice details: if you ask for a company invoice, the company name, business or VAT number and address.
  • Newsletter: if you subscribe to flight deals, your email address and preferred language.
  • Price alerts: your email address, the route, dates, number of travellers and the price shown, so that we can tell you when the price drops.
  • Group requests: your name, email, phone, the route, dates, number of travellers and your notes, so that we can prepare a group offer.
  • BUNAVIA Club waiting list: your email address and preferred language, so that we can tell you when the Club opens.
  • Payment details: handled by our payment provider once payments open. We do not plan to keep full card numbers.
  • Technical data: your IP address and basic browser information, which reach us and our service providers when you open the site.
  • Your choices: the language and currency you choose, stored in your own browser.

4. Why we use it and on what basis

  • To answer your request and give you fares: your request or steps before a contract.
  • To create and run your customer account: the agreement you accept when you register.
  • To make, issue and manage a booking: performing the contract with you.
  • To keep records, issue invoices and meet legal duties: legal obligation.
  • To keep the website secure and working, and to show prices in a suitable currency: our legitimate interests.
  • To send flight deals by email: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time by replying to any offer email or writing to info@bunavia.com.
  • For price alerts and the Club waiting list: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time by writing to info@bunavia.com.
  • For group requests: steps taken at your request before a contract (preparing an offer).
  • To show a booking request in "Manage booking": anyone who enters the booking reference together with a passenger's last name sees the trip, status and total, but no passenger details. Keep your booking reference private.

5. Who receives your data

  • Airlines and, once connected, the booking and ticketing systems we use, such as the Travelport GDS, so that your ticket can be issued.
  • Our payment provider (a bank or payment company), which processes card, Apple Pay and Google Pay payments, once online payment is active. to confirm: provider name
  • Supabase, which hosts our customer accounts and database, including agency requests. Our Supabase project is located in the European Union (Paris, France).
  • Service providers that carry your messages or host our website: our website host (GitHub Pages), and our email provider.
  • Resend (Resend Inc.), which sends our booking and e-ticket emails. It receives your email address and the content of the email.
  • tawk.to (tawk.to inc.), which runs our live chat. If you open the live chat, the messages you write, your name or email if you give them, and technical data such as your IP address and browser are processed by tawk.to so that our agents can answer you.
  • Anthropic (Anthropic PBC), which provides the AI behind the “BUNAVIA assistant” chat. If you use the assistant, the questions you write and its answers are sent to Anthropic to produce the answer. We do not store the conversation; it stays only in your browser until you close the tab. To limit misuse we keep, for three days, a one-way code made from your IP address and the time of each question. If you give a booking reference and last name, the assistant looks up only the status, route, dates and total of that booking. Please do not write card, passport or other sensitive details in the assistant.
  • jsDelivr, the content network that delivers the software library used for accounts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.
  • Google Fonts, which supplies the fonts used by this site and so receives your IP address.
  • Frankfurter (frankfurter.dev), which supplies the current exchange rates for showing prices in CHF, USD or GBP, and so receives your IP address when the page loads. Rates are reference rates; payment is always in euro.
  • A country-lookup service (api.country.is), which receives your IP address so that we can show Swiss visitors prices in CHF. We do not store the result.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

Cookies policy

Cookies are small files that a website stores on your device. At this time this website does not use analytics or advertising cookies.

What the website stores in your browser

  • Your language choice and your currency choice, so you do not have to choose again. These are small preference settings kept in your browser. They are not used for tracking.
  • If you log in, a login session (a secure token) so that you stay logged in. It is removed when you log out. This is strictly necessary for the account to work.

Third-party services

  • Google Fonts, jsDelivr and the country-lookup service receive your IP address when the page loads, as described in the privacy policy.
  • Live chat: the chat window from tawk.to loads only when you choose “Live chat”. From then on, tawk.to stores cookies and local data on your device to keep your conversation open. If you do not open the live chat, no chat cookies are set.
  • Map: the map of our office comes from Google Maps and loads only when you click “Show our office on the map”. From then on, Google receives your IP address and may set its own cookies. If you do not click, nothing is loaded from Google Maps.
  • Links that open your email or phone app leave our website. They follow their own cookie and privacy rules.

If this changes

If we add analytics, advertising or similar tools, we will update this page and ask for your consent first where the law requires it.

Your control

You can delete stored data or block cookies in your browser settings. If you clear site data, the site will forget your language and currency choice.

Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to use our website. We build it so that it works on phones and computers, can be used with a keyboard, uses readable text and clear colour contrast, and is available in English, German, French and Albanian. The legal pages are currently in English only.

If you find something that is hard to use, tell us at info@bunavia.com, and we will try to fix it or help you another way.
