Questions travellers ask

Can I book flights with connections?

Yes. By default we show direct and connecting flights. Tick “Direct flights only” to see only direct flights. We show the connection airport and total travel time so you can compare.

Why is the price shown in CHF, USD or GBP if I pay in euro?

The converted price is an approximate guide in your own currency. Your invoice and payment are in euro, and your bank may apply its own exchange rate.

How do I get my ticket?

After payment we issue the ticket and email the e-ticket to you.

Can I change or cancel a booking?

That depends on the airline’s fare rules for your ticket. Contact us and we will check the options and handle the request.

What baggage is included in my ticket?

It depends on the airline and the fare. Before you book, the fare conditions show what is included, usually a small bag. You can add a checked bag (23 kg) during booking. For exact sizes and weights, check the airline’s website; the link is on your e-ticket.

Which travel documents do I need?

You need a valid passport or ID card that is accepted for your trip, and a visa if your nationality and destination require one, also for connections. Entry rules change, so check official sources before you travel, for example the EU’s official site travel-europe.europa.eu for the Schengen area.

How do I travel with children and babies?

Book every child with their date of birth. Children under 2 travel as infants on an adult’s lap; children from 2 to 11 have their own seat. Every child needs their own travel document. If a child travels without parents, contact us before booking, because each airline has its own rules.

Can I travel with my pet?

It depends on the airline, the animal and the route, and places for pets are limited. Contact us before you book so that we can check it with the airline and add your pet to the booking.

When and how do I check in?

Online check-in usually opens 24 to 48 hours before departure on the airline’s website. Use the airline booking code from your e-ticket. We also email you a reminder the day before your flight.

Which payment methods can I use?

Card (Visa, Mastercard), Apple Pay, Google Pay, bank transfer in euro, or payment at our office in Prishtina. Online card payment is being set up: until then an agent contacts you to complete the payment. The ticket is issued after payment.
