Questions travellers ask

Can I book flights with connections?

Yes. Choose direct only, up to one stop, or any connections. We show the connection airport and total travel time so you can compare.

Why is the price shown in CHF, USD or GBP if I pay in euro?

The converted price is an approximate guide in your own currency. Your invoice and payment are in euro, and your bank may apply its own exchange rate.

How do I get my ticket?

After payment we issue the ticket and email the e-ticket to you.

Can I change or cancel a booking?

That depends on the airline’s fare rules for your ticket. Contact us and we will check the options and handle the request.
